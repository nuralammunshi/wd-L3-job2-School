WHO WE ARE

A little school with a big heart.

Our story is one of caring teachers, curious students, and a community growing together.

OUR STORY

Rooted in community.
Growing since 1998.

Shapla Model School began with a simple belief: every child deserves a place where they feel seen, supported, and inspired to learn.

From our first classroom to the vibrant school we are today, that belief has guided everything we do. Generations of students have found confidence, friendship, and possibility here. We keep evolving with our community while holding on to what matters most: thoughtful teaching, open minds, and a sense of belonging.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our mission & vision.

Our vision
